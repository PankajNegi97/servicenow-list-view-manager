# Privacy Policy for List View Manager for ServiceNow

**Effective Date:** August 18, 2026

## 1. Overview
**List View Manager for ServiceNow** ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use our Chrome Extension.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and Store
- **Local Column Profiles**: The extension saves your user-created column layout names and column lists locally in your web browser using standard browser storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **No External Data Transmission**: All data remains 100% strictly local to your browser. We do **NOT** collect, track, transmit, upload, or store any personal data, ServiceNow instance data, credentials, session tokens, or browsing history on external servers.

---

## 3. Permissions Used
The extension requests only minimal permissions required for core functionality:
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to save and retrieve your custom column profiles locally on your device.
- **`activeTab` & `scripting`**: Used to run column arrangement scripts in the active ServiceNow tab when you click "Apply" or "Capture".
- **`host_permissions` (`*://*.service-now.com/*`, `*://*.servicenow.com/*`)**: Used to identify ServiceNow slushbucket UI elements on official ServiceNow domain pages.

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing and Sale of Data
- We do **NOT** sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties.
- We do **NOT** use tracking analytics, telemetry scripts, or third-party ad networks.
